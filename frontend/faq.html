Frequently Asked Questions

What does NeuroLens detect?

NeuroLens screens MRI images for brain tumor, stroke, and Alzheimer-related risk using multiple trained AI models.

How do I use the system?

Upload your MRI image, wait for the analysis to finish, then open the disease result pages for detailed predictions and visual GradCAM explanations.

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. NeuroLens is a support tool that provides AI-based screening information. Always confirm with a qualified medical professional.

Which image formats are supported?

The interface accepts common MRI image formats such as PNG, JPG, JPEG, and DICOM where supported by the browser upload flow.

Why is explainability important?

GradCAM overlays help show which image regions influenced the model predictions, increasing transparency for each result.

About Us

NeuroLens was built to make advanced brain imaging analysis more approachable, combining AI screening with clear results and visual evidence.

Our goal is to support doctors and patients with intelligent MRI insights while keeping the interface simple, fast, and easy to use.
